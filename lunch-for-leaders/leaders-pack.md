# The Leaders' Pack — QR deliverable

The added value for the 27 August lunch. Guests scan a QR code on the table
and download a pack. It exists because nobody in that room has time to act on
the day, so they take it home instead.

**Status:** specified, not built. Target: ready in the next one to two weeks,
well ahead of 27 August.

> **Positioning caution.** This is not a finished product yet. Present it as a
> set of working drafts shared with the room, not as a polished commercial
> release. That is honest and it lowers the bar for getting it out.

---

## Mechanics

```
QR on table  →  simple landing page  →  name + email  →  pack download
                                            ↓
                                  warm leads, captured
```

The email capture is the commercial point. It turns a lunch into a follow-up
list of leaders who have self-identified as interested in AI governance.

**Keep the friction low.** Name, email, company. Nothing else. Anything more
and people abandon it at a lunch table.

---

## Contents

| # | Item | Purpose |
|---|------|---------|
| 1 | **Workshop promo, one page** | The face to face leaders' governance and management workshop. The commercial ask |
| 2 | **Draft AI Use Policy** | Template. What staff may and may not do with AI |
| 3 | **AI Risk Assessment** | Template. Structured way to record where AI creates exposure |
| 4 | **AI Impact Assessment** | Template. Consequences of deployment, including on people |
| 5 | **Governance Checklist** | The quick self assessment. Also the hook back to the online version |

Package as **one branded PDF** with the templates as separate editable files.
One download, not five. A leader who has to click five times will click none.

---

## Build order

1. **Governance Checklist** first. It is the shortest, it doubles as the talk's
   punchline, and it drives the self assessment.
2. **Workshop promo.** Needed for the commercial return.
3. **AI Use Policy.** The one most likely to be used immediately.
4. **Risk and Impact Assessments.** The heaviest, and the most tolerant of
   being labelled draft.
5. **Landing page and QR.** Last, once there is something to download.

---

## Guardrails

Per the confidentiality rules on Integr8te material:

- **No pricing** anywhere in the pack. The workshop page invites a
  conversation, it does not quote a number.
- **No methodology.** The pack gives templates and the checklist. It does not
  explain the assessment framework behind them.
- The **secure versus credible** reframe may be used as a hook in the talk and
  on the workshop page. Do not explain the method underneath it.
