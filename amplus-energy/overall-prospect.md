## Why the prospect is attractive

1. **Sovereign-backed** — Timor-Leste's first 100% sovereign oil development;
   the state partner (TIMOR GAP) is inside the JV *and* co-funding capex, which
   de-risks both politics and money.
2. **Proven host vessel** — redeploying an existing, "most-deployed" FPSO with
   *limited* modifications is far cheaper and faster than a newbuild floater.
3. **Hub with running room** — the ~23 MMbbl of additional upside can be tied
   back to the same hub, turning a single project into a **production platform**.
4. **Fast cycle** — FID to first oil in ~15–18 months is quick for offshore.
5. **Capital already flowing** — placements, equity raise, long-lead
   procurement and a portfolio farm-out (P2530 → Serica) are done, not planned.

## Key risks

| Risk | Comment |
|------|---------|
| **Debt financing** | Not yet closed; the primary FID gate. |
| **Rig availability** | Rig-share LOI (SundaGas) must firm up in a tight market. |
| **FPSO life-extension execution** | 30+ yr-old asset — upgrade scope, integrity and schedule risk on redeployment. |
| **Oil price / schedule** | Small-cap economics are price-sensitive; slippage pushes first-oil cash flow. |
| **Single-asset concentration** | Finder's near-term value is heavily KTJ-weighted. |

## Verdict

A **credibly de-risked, near-term development** whose remaining hurdles are
**commercial (debt + rig + contracts)** rather than technical or political.
The FPSO redeployment model is the crux — and the crux is exactly where
integrity, marine and life-extension services are bought.

## Our angle — Integr8te / MAKO / ScrubMarine

**The hook:** a 30-year-old FPSO being life-extended and redeployed is a
textbook **asset-integrity + marine-services** programme — and Amplus is
selling *capability and O&M*, not just a hull, so they need exactly this
supply chain. The right time to be in the conversation is **before FID locks
the execution vendors.**

| Our capability | Fit on KTJ / Petrojarl I |
|----------------|--------------------------|
| **MAKO Offshore — LICHA** (penetration / barrier / integrity intelligence) | Life-extension of an old FPSO = re-baselining penetrations, seals and fire/blast boundaries. LICHA turns fragmented legacy records into an evidenced register for the duty holder — directly relevant to reclass and redeployment. |
| **ScrubMarine** (hull / marine services) | Hull condition, cleaning and marine husbandry on a vessel being reactivated and towed to station, then kept on station for years. |
| **Integr8te** (data / digital integration) | Wrap the redeployment and O&M phase in a single data spine; Amplus is standing up a *new* operating organisation and needs systems, not spreadsheets. |
| **Decarbonisation** | Amplus's Carbon Circle / offshore-electrification agenda is an adjacency we can align to. |

### Suggested follow-through
1. **Map the decision-makers** at Amplus (Aberdeen — home turf) and Finder;
   Amplus is the warmer, geographically closer entry point.
2. **Lead with the FPSO life-extension pain** (integrity re-baseline +
   produced-water upgrade + reactivation), not a generic capability pitch.
3. **Position pre-FID** so we're in the frame as the execution supply chain
   forms around the mid-2026 / Q3-2026 decision.
4. **Treat Amplus as a repeatable channel** — West Africa (VG Energy) is the
   next redeploy, so a win on KTJ is a template, not a one-off.
