### The 10 minute talk — content spine

Not a pitch. The value is that they leave knowing where they stand.

1. **The gap.** Most businesses are already using AI without any governance
   around it. Leaders carry the accountability whether or not they set it up.
2. **What good looks like.** The four things a board actually needs: a use
   policy, a risk assessment, an impact assessment, and a checklist to prove
   it is being run.
3. **Where leaders get caught out.** Short, concrete, from experience.
4. **Know where you stand.** Point at the self-assessment.
5. **Scan the code.** Everything just described is in the pack, drafted and
   ready to adapt. Nothing to do now, take it home.

**Tone rule:** this is a favour to the room, not a sales slot. The pack does
the selling afterwards.
