# Amplus Energy — Company Profile

## Who they are

**Amplus Energy Services Ltd** is an **Aberdeen (Scotland) based floating
production solutions specialist**. Its model has historically favoured
**field-development solutions, vessel design and leasing** over outright
ownership — i.e. selling *capability and engineering*, not just steel. The
2026 acquisition of the Petrojarl I marks a deliberate shift toward becoming a
**fully operational FPSO operator**, building out its own onshore support and
offshore crews.

## Core capability

- **Field development solutions** — concept through FEED, redeployment and
  life-extension of existing floating production units.
- **FPSO ownership / leasing** — flexible commercial models for producers who
  don't want to own the floater.
- **Operations & maintenance** — standing up the onshore/offshore
  organisation to run vessels on station.

## Fleet — the Petrojarl I FPSO

- Acquired by Amplus earlier in 2026 and marketed as **"the most flexible and
  most deployed FPSO in history."**
- Refurbished / prepared for **redeployment with limited modifications**; the
  core separation and processing systems are largely **fit for purpose**, with
  the main identified upgrade being a **produced-water treatment system**.
- Engineering confirms redeployment can deliver **~25,000–30,000 bopd**.
- Transition supported by a partnership with **V.Ships Offshore**.

## Carbon-capture arm

Amplus has pushed into carbon capture to complement its field-development and
FPSO business:

- Multi-million-pound investment via **Carbon Circle** (Carbon Circle AS is
  established in Norwegian and Canadian CCS; **Carbon Circle UK** is running
  **FEED for the decarbonisation of two major UK industrial sites**).
- **MOU with Ocean-Power AS and Veri Energy** to cut the carbon footprint of
  offshore petroleum activity through offshore electrification + carbon
  capture.

## Read-across

Amplus is assembling a **redeploy-and-operate** platform: buy a proven
floater, life-extend it, place it on a real field (KTJ), and monetise via
FEED + O&M while holding equity upside in the operator. That is a
**recurring, multi-asset** playbook — West Africa is explicitly next — which
makes them a **repeatable counterparty**, not a one-off, for anyone in the
integrity / marine-services / decarbonisation supply chain.
