Why it's attractive
  • Sovereign-backed — state partner TIMOR GAP is inside the JV and co-funding capex.
  • Proven host vessel — redeploying an existing FPSO beats a newbuild on cost and schedule.
  • Hub with running room — ~23 MMbbl upside can tie back to the same hub.
  • Fast cycle — FID to first oil in ~15–18 months.
  • Capital already flowing — placements, raise, long-lead and a farm-out are done, not planned.
Key risks
  • Debt financing not yet closed — the primary FID gate.
  • Rig availability — LOI must firm up in a tight regional market.
  • FPSO life-extension — 30+ yr asset; integrity & schedule risk on redeployment.
  • Price / schedule — small-cap economics are oil-price sensitive.
  • Concentration — Finder's near-term value is heavily KTJ-weighted.
