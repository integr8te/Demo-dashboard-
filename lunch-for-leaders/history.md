# Lunch4Leaders — Background and History

## What it is

An invitation-only lunch for Aberdeen energy and business leaders, hosted by
Grant Blackaby under the Integr8te banner. Small table, good food, genuine
conversation. No pitches, no panels.

The governing rules Grant set for the guest list:

- **No operators and no regulators.** Steve Gardyne was treated as the
  exception.
- Invitees should **benefit and provide mutual value**, checked name by name.
- Keep it small enough to **create a little FOMO**.

## Event 1

The first lunch ran and drew, in Grant's words, "so much amazing and positive
feedback." It set up the second.

### Why it failed

Not lack of interest. The killer was **two date moves inside three weeks**
landing on top of the summer holiday season, which shredded availability down
to 7. The lesson for the relaunch: **fix the date once, then hold it.**

### Assets from June

- `Integr8te_Lunch4Leaders_June2026.pdf` — the flyer
- `Integr8te_Lunch4Leaders_June2026_2.jpg` — the specially curated menu
- Both exist **only as email attachments** on the 25 May invite. Neither is
  saved in Google Drive. They could not be opened in session, so the August
  flyer is a fresh rebuild rather than an edit of the original.

### Payment mechanics used

- Card / Apple Pay, plus a **Monzo link** as fallback:
  `monzo.com/pay/r/integr8te-limited_UYdlvROk2Unxy1`
- No Eventbrite or ticketing platform. This is worth revisiting for the
  relaunch, since manual payment tracking is what makes the refund
  reconciliation painful now.

## What changes for August

1. **One date, held.** Thursday 27 August.
2. **Hard cap of 16** rather than an open ~18, to drive scarcity and make the
   numbers predictable for the venue.
3. **Added value on the day** so attending is worth more than the lunch: the
   10 minute AI for Leaders briefing and the QR-delivered Leaders' Pack.
4. **Priority for those who already paid** in June, as goodwill for the
   cancellation.
