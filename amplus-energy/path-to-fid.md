# KTJ — The Path to FID

**Project:** Kuda Tasi & Jahal (KTJ) oilfields, PSC 19-11, Timor Sea,
offshore **Timor-Leste** — the country's **first 100% sovereign oil
development**.
**Operator:** Finder Energy (ASX: FDR) **66%** · **TIMOR GAP 34%**
**FID target:** mid-2026 / Q3 2026 · **First oil:** late 2027 – early 2028

---

## Development concept

A **hub-based subsea development** with wells tied back to the **Petrojarl I
FPSO**, engineered so that future discoveries in the licence can be tied in to
the same hub. Target production **~25,000–30,000 bopd**.

## What still gates FID (the watch-list)

1. **Debt package closed** — the biggest single dependency; capex is
   part-covered by TIMOR GAP (50% to US$338m) but the balance needs debt.
2. **Rig secured** — the SundaGas rig-share LOI must convert to a firm slot in
   a tight regional rig market.
3. **EPC / long-lead contracts** signed — including the FPSO
   life-extension/upgrade scope (produced-water treatment) that Amplus leads.
4. **Final regulatory / JV sign-offs** feeding the FID paper.

**Interpretation:** the *subsurface, resource, host-vessel and partner*
questions are essentially answered. The remaining gates are **commercial and
contractual** (money + rig + contracts). That is why the update reads as
"advancing to FID" rather than "assessing feasibility."

---

## Amplus's role through FID and beyond

Amplus is **not** a pure vendor that walks away after selling the floater. Per
the agreement it remains central to execution:

- **FEED** for the redeployment,
- **life-extension & upgrade scopes** on Petrojarl I,
- **operations & maintenance** once the vessel is on station,
- and it holds **equity in Finder**, aligning it to first oil and beyond.

This is the thread to follow: the FPSO life-extension + O&M scope is a
**multi-year integrity-and-marine programme** that spins up *around* FID.
