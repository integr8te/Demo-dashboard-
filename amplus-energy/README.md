## Executive summary (60 seconds)

- **Amplus Energy Services Ltd** is an **Aberdeen-based floating-production
  specialist**. In 2026 it bought the **Petrojarl I FPSO** — "the most
  deployed FPSO in history" — refurbished it for redeployment, then **sold it
  to Finder Energy for US$15m (cash + shares)** and took a **significant
  equity stake in Finder**. Amplus stays central to execution: FEED,
  life-extension/upgrade scopes, and later **operations & maintenance** once
  the vessel is on station.
- The FPSO is destined for Finder's flagship **KTJ project offshore
  Timor-Leste** — the country's **first 100% sovereign oil development**.
  Finder operates **66%**, state oil company **TIMOR GAP holds 34%** and funds
  **50% of development capex up to a US$338m gross cap**.
- **RISC-certified 2C resources ~25.5 MMbbl**, plus ~23 MMbbl further upside
  in the licence. Target production **~25,000–30,000 bopd** through Petrojarl I.
- **FDP approved (July 2026)**, development area approved, funding and
  long-lead items underway. **FID targeted mid-2026 / Q3 2026; first oil late
  2027–early 2028.**
